# Rekky: Flutter product and delivery plan

Updated: 2026-09-26. Status: product decisions revised after review; repository established; F-00.1, F-00.3 and the F-01.1 transcription bridge are in progress. The Rust backend, Flutter shell and shared fixtures exist; manual text and real local recording have been exercised on a physical Android phone. No delivery phase has passed its full gate. F-00 resolves the remaining setup choices and measures the proposed quality/performance gates.

This is the active product and implementation reference. It supersedes the previous experience, capture, voice and completion plans wherever they differ. Legacy documents and code are technical references only: no consent, deletion, retention, guest, audience or delivery rule is inherited implicitly. This document defines those policies for the new product. The existing browser prototype is an interaction inventory, not the approved design; see [legacy reference and provenance](./LEGACY_REFERENCE.md).

## 1. Product decision

Rekky is a personal and network-visible store of useful knowledge from people. A person contributes something naturally; Rekky preserves it, understands it, organizes it and helps their Rekky friends retrieve it in context. Completed knowledge is available to the user's reciprocal friends automatically; sharing each recommendation is not a separate chore. Recommendations are an output of this knowledge, not a mandatory form people must complete.

Product promise: **Tell Rekky once. Find it when it matters. Pass it on effortlessly.**

The finished interaction is the product test: after sign-in, open Rekky, tap once, speak freely and leave. Rekky temporarily holds the recording, saves a private text transcript, distills useful claims, organizes them and makes them retrievable. Later, a natural question produces a concise answer and the right action from the user's own knowledge and authorized network contributions. Delete the audio promptly after a usable transcript is durably committed; extraction and enrichment continue from text. Retain the transcript as an owner-only recovery/correction source until the owner deletes it or the account. Transcripts are never friend-visible, and V1 has no processed-audio archive or playback.

The experience should be simple, pleasurable and visually expressive. AI earns its place by removing work: identifying several subjects in a recording, keeping their advice separate, remembering attribution, organizing them appropriately, resolving useful destinations and answering a later need with evidence. Adding a chat box or animation to a conventional organizer does not establish this outcome.

### Scope of the first release

Support everyday knowledge: experiences, recommendations, warnings, things to try, useful people, places, products, media, activities, tools and general advice. Preserve unclear or unstructured material without forcing it into a recommendation. Voice is the primary capture input. Shared text/URLs arrive in F-02; a typed recommendation form is removed from the mobile experience.

**Retrieval order and source clarity:** Ask looks first in the user's own knowledge and completed knowledge from their reciprocal Rekky friends. The product discloses this network visibility before account use; an individual share action is not required. If that evidence is thin, Rekky may offer public information or provider leads as a clearly separate option, subject to the user's external-search choice and configured providers. Public findings are labelled with their actual source; they are not presented as a trusted person's recommendation. A factual lookup that verifies the identity or destination of an already saved item is a distinct operation from discovering new public options. Neither operation may manufacture an endorsement.

The long-term knowledge-store ambition can accommodate other inputs. Full document archives, automatic message ingestion, screenshot OCR, imported audio, enterprise search and a general autonomous personal assistant are later extensions. They must not delay reliable capture and retrieval of the first useful memory.

### What success feels like

Someone with no Rekky friends can save a messy thought in seconds, leave, and later recover the useful information without remembering its exact wording. Someone receiving that advice can act on it, understand where it came from, and keep it with attribution. Users never need to maintain categories, tags or polished posts to make this work.

### Decision status and first learning milestone

**Fixed product decisions:** sign-in is required; there is no guest mode. Ask is home, Library is secondary and Remember is always available after sign-in. Completed items default to Friends after the account disclosure is accepted; Private remains a simple per-item or per-capture override. Keep owner-only text transcripts and delete processed audio. Use a fresh Flutter client and Rust/Postgres backend in this standalone repository, with no legacy users or data.

**Measured implementation choices:** exact palette, typography, shelf labels, native packages, routing/schema-generation tools, model/provider selection, retrieval technique, latency targets and resource limits are selected or adjusted with evidence. The initial one-understanding-call path is an optimization target subordinate to faithful extraction, not a reason to reject a safer measured fallback.

**Setup decisions still open:** release signing and store identity, iOS device and macOS runner access, hosting/database/storage configuration, provider-specific processing/retention disclosures and named pilot operations ownership. The Android debug identity and Google sign-in client are configured for local development; that does not establish release credentials or production hosting. Resolve each open choice before its dependent implementation or rollout gate.

The first learning milestone is a signed-in person saving several real, messy thoughts and later recovering useful advice, warnings and uncertainty without remembering exact wording. Recruit an initial English/Hindi/Hinglish cohort with everyday place and service needs; other useful knowledge remains savable. Assess solo usefulness before network expansion, then test small groups of existing friends. Successful processing alone is not evidence of later usefulness.

## 2. Project and technology decision

**Build a new Flutter application from scratch in `apps/rekky_flutter/` in the standalone `rekky-app` repository.** It is an independent Dart project with its own dependencies, tests, build configuration and release pipeline. It does not import the React Native UI, pager, state containers or JavaScript capture implementation.

Keep the new Flutter app, new backend and language-neutral contracts together in this standalone repository, with independent Git history and GitHub management. The existing `mapx` repository is a read-only reference for this build; it is not a build, runtime or deployment dependency. Bring across audited adapters and invariants selectively, with new-product tests and without legacy domain imports. Do not copy old user data into the new product. This supersedes the earlier decision to begin inside the legacy repository, following the user's explicit repository decision on 2026-09-25.

**Build a separate, small Rust/Postgres backend application in `apps/rekky_backend/` in this standalone repository, with its own deployment, database, migrations, jobs, account IDs, API and contract.** Use one Rust codebase with bounded API and worker process roles, not a service split by default. Do not route the new app through the old backend or point it at old product tables. A familiar Google/Apple identity may create a fresh account in the new database, never resolve to an old account ID. Keep the current mobile app, backend and data operational and untouched while piloting the new app under a distinct app/store identity. This is a clean product foundation with selective reuse, not a rewrite of every integration: extract or adapt proven transcription, asset-storage and lookup adapters, evidence checks, budget/lease patterns and contract tests behind new Rust interfaces. Do not copy old recommendation, search, social or capture routes and workers wholesale; their data and visibility assumptions differ from this plan.

The architecture review found roughly 32,000 backend TypeScript lines and 36 migrations. The largest recommendation route exceeds 2,000 lines; old visibility defaults to followers, search is shaped around places/services and small result limits, and capture workers join old account/private-capture tables. Those are product mismatches, not proof the existing backend is broken: it type-checks and focused voice-module tests pass. The full test suite could not be assessed in this sandbox because contract tests bind a local server. Favor a new backend now because everyone starts with no old data, so migration provides no user benefit. Start with one vertical slice—new account, temporary voice, distilled item, own Ask—and add reciprocal-friend access before the first network pilot. Preserve the old backend unchanged except for separately tested, backward-compatible shared-code extraction.

Flutter is the selected implementation framework. Its suitability for this product still depends on proving recording, file recovery, contacts, share entry and accessibility on both platforms. It does not itself remove native lifecycle or synchronization issues. Follow Flutter's separation of views, presentation state and repositories; keep business operations out of widgets. [Flutter architecture recommendations](https://docs.flutter.dev/app-architecture/recommendations)

## 3. Application shape

Use three distinct experiences: **Ask (the home)**, **Remember (focused recording)** and **Library**. Keep Ask and Library as stable destinations with a central, labelled Remember action that opens the recording experience and starts listening in the same tap. Remember is an action rather than a tab that requires a second microphone tap. Activity is a labelled secondary entry for genuine requests and replies; account and preferences remain secondary. Keep these positions stable, including while processing finishes. This supersedes the earlier two-tab-plus-form prototype following the user's 2026-09-26 UX decision.

### Sign-in and account entry

Sign-in is required before capturing, browsing, asking or saving shared text/URLs. Support Google/Apple identity through the new account system, verifying provider tokens on the backend and issuing new-product sessions. There are no anonymous accounts, guest libraries or guest-import flows. A first sign-in requires connectivity. Once signed in, the same locally authenticated account can capture and browse its own synchronized material offline; expired remote credentials pause network work until reauthentication. Sign-out locks the account's local store and clears credentials; no other account or signed-out surface can read it. Account deletion purges it on the next permitted device execution. Do not imply an offline phone instantly learns remote session revocation.

Before the account enters the product, explain that completed recommendations are available to accepted friends, including earlier Friends items and deliberately attached provider contacts. Explain in the same concise setup that Remember automatically sends temporary audio and private transcript text to the named processors, deletes accepted audio, and saves extracted knowledge. The affirmative Continue enables the separately recorded voice and understanding permissions; declining leaves the user at setup, with sign-out/delete-account available. Ask for microphone, contacts and location only when used. Do not show voice/transcript processing dialogs during ordinary capture. Previously withdrawn processing is never silently enabled again; the user can opt in explicitly from settings. Processing permissions in section 8 remain separate from account identity and Friends visibility.

A share entry received while signed out opens sign-in and asks the person to share again afterward; it must not silently retain or upload the payload in a guest queue. Once authenticated, share entry uses the same account-bound capture/outbox service.

### Ask home

After sign-in, the home screen is a calm launch surface for the two core intents: “What do you need?” and “Remember this.” A voice/text Ask entry and a prominent, labelled Remember action are immediately available; neither requires opening Library first. The app must make the distinction between asking and saving unmistakable. The first screen is not a blank chatbot, a mixed recommendation feed, a collection grid or an AI activity dashboard.

Use predictable slots with little copy. If a recording is processing, one quiet status can show what is pending without trapping the user. If Rekky has genuinely useful saved context, it may offer at most one relevant way back into that knowledge, grounded in real data rather than invented prompts or arbitrary recommendations. This same optional slot may offer one high-value completion on a later visit, such as attaching a tutor's contact; it does not add another panel or open a sheet automatically. An empty account emphasizes a first thought to save while still allowing Ask; an established account can emphasize asking without moving or hiding Remember. Do not change the navigation hierarchy based on how many items exist. Library remains one tap away.

### Library

Library is an organized, secondary collection of knowledge for browsing, inspecting and correcting. Its default scope is **Your Library**: items the user created plus friends' items they explicitly chose to keep. A kept item is a reference that preserves its original author and current network access, not a new endorsement or a private copy that survives removal of access. The wider network's available knowledge belongs in Ask and a clearly distinct **From your network** view within Library, not a third primary destination; it is never dumped into Your Library merely because the user can access it. A compact search entry and Remember remain available. Library does not open as a mixed feed of unrelated objects or a dashboard of processing settings.

- Empty library: one inviting prompt and Remember. No fabricated content or empty category grid.
- Sparse library: show the populated shelf and its useful contents directly. One save must not create an elaborate filing system.
- Established library: stable shelf order, meaningful previews and a small optional Recently added entry. Recent chronology is a secondary view; dynamic context views do not replace the stable shelves.
- Shelf previews use a meaningful example or permitted image and a short label. Places can use supported imagery and locality; people/services favor the job, area and contact route. Avoid repeating identical tiles or printing summaries of everything in a shelf.
- Within a shelf, show the item once as a compact tappable card: name, shelf/locality and a small audience indicator. Do not show the summary or source passages in Library previews. A short Caution, Needs review, Heard from others or Not tried yet signal can preserve an important distinction; the complete summary, caveats, provenance, useful destination and corrections open on tap. Aim for substantially more visible items without fixed heights or reduced accessibility text sizes. Show only filters that make sense for that shelf, and no deep folder tree.
- A visible city or area view can lead when relevant, but **never silently remove other cities or unknown locations from the user's Library**. Provide an obvious All locations route. For services, local views use supported service-area coverage, not a past-job location. A city inferred from permitted foreground location is optional, approximate and never overwrites a user-selected city; denied permission leaves manual city choice and All locations available. Changing the phone's location cannot move or hide saved items.
- From your network uses a visible source and location scope, with attribution and current reciprocal-friend checks before counts/previews. Keeping a friend's recommendation adds one reference to Your Library; it does not duplicate the underlying item or broaden its audience.
- Search inside a collection inherits that scope and makes it visible. Broadening to all knowledge is an explicit action.
- Preserve scroll position, focus and collection order as enrichment arrives. New information must not move the item being touched.

### Remember

Tap the labelled Remember action to start recording immediately after the device microphone permission is available. The recording screen has one short experience prompt, elapsed time, Done and Discard; there is no typing choice, title, rating, category or audience picker. The app never starts listening on launch or ordinary navigation. A short gesture path and haptics support one-handed use, with equivalent labelled controls for accessibility. Text and URL share entry remains a separate future F-02 input path, not a choice inside Remember.

The normal path is one tap to start speaking, then Done. Done acknowledges that the recording is durably queued on the account's device, not that the recommendation has already been extracted. The app automatically uploads, transcribes, deletes accepted audio, extracts and saves without another capture decision. It resumes unfinished local work after restart and uses bounded server jobs so leaving a screen does not strand processing. Show one quiet, accurate pending state in Library; show an actionable failure only when automatic recovery cannot finish. On later visits, the distilled knowledge is the saved object; there is no archive or replay of processed voice notes. An ungrounded source-preserving fallback remains private and visibly needs review, never a finished friend recommendation.

### Ask

Ask starts on the home through voice or text and opens a focused answer view. The current intent is visible: recording a question does not silently create a library entry. A question can be kept explicitly; a capture can be corrected or searched from its detail. Avoid forcing a mode-selection form before every input.

Show a short orientation and every genuinely relevant recommendation, ranked so the strongest are easiest to scan. One top result may have a richer preview, but other qualifying results remain visible or reachable by scrolling and loading more; never silently reduce an answer to one winner or a fixed top-three. Offer My knowledge / Shared with me / Both scopes, with a remembered preference and a visible effective scope. If those sources have insufficient coverage, offer “Explore public sources” as a separate path, with its source and recency visible. Do not silently merge public leads into the trusted result set. Local results arrive before an optional generated explanation. Conversation history is available when useful, but does not become the library or the default home.

For “a cab service in Bangalore,” show a relevant saved or shared service with the actual experience, attribution and supported contact route. For a guide, show the source person's advice and booking/contact route if known. If trusted knowledge is sparse, explain that gap and offer public options in a visibly separate section or follow-up. A public listing has its own source and destination; it never inherits a friend's praise or the trust attached to a network contribution.

### Activity and sharing

Requests and replies are accessible without dominating everyday capture. An answer can be kept; an existing knowledge item can be used in a reply. There is no AI task inbox or completion backlog.

### Connections and the empty-network start

Rekky must be useful before the user has a single friend: their own saved knowledge works in Ask, and optional public discovery remains clearly separate. Do not invent starter friends, require invites during signup or block the first save behind contact permission. After a useful save, a sparse Ask result or an explicit Friends entry, offer one calm way to bring in people whose judgment the user trusts. A need-led invitation (“Ask Priya for a good tailor”) can be more meaningful than a generic growth prompt, but sending it is always the user's action; never auto-message contacts.

Keep the visibility explanation short and timed to the actual decision. Account setup explains Friends-by-default and requires the affirmative action above. At the **first friend acceptance**, repeat briefly that existing and future Friends items, including attached provider contacts, become accessible and offer **Review what friends will see** before Accept or Not now. Not now leaves the signed-in person able to use their own knowledge. Do not add a per-save approval form or repeatedly challenge the agreed sharing model. Show the effective Friends/Private audience unobtrusively during capture and on item detail; users may make an item Private after creation, with the synchronization limits in section 8. Ask for contact access only when the user elects contact-based finding. Validate disclosure comprehension and ease of correcting mistakes as implementation quality, while retaining Friends-by-default as the selected product behavior.

Use Rekky's own **reciprocal, accepted connection** as the friend relationship because completed Friends items become visible in both directions, including earlier eligible items. An invite/request displays that consequence before acceptance. A one-way follow, a phone-book entry, a matching email/number or use of the same identity provider is not friendship. Provide exact handle/profile search for discoverable existing members, a user-initiated invite link through the phone's native share sheet, and optional selection of individual phone contacts. If a selected contact matches a Rekky member who opted into discoverability, offer a connection request; otherwise let the user send an invite. Do not upload a whole address book, retain nonmember contact data for graph building, expose a member who chose not to be discoverable, or send invitations automatically. If contact access is denied, handle search and invite links still work. Rate-limit invitations and lookups; support decline, remove and block without revealing private content in previews or error messages.

### Trust, reporting and account control

Friends-by-default makes a mistaken recommendation or unwanted provider contact visible to other people. Before any external friends pilot, each item and attached contact needs a clear **Report a problem** action with concise reasons such as incorrect/outdated information, unwanted personal contact information and abuse; a person can also report or block another account. A report is not another recommendation and does not publish the reporter's identity to the author. Let the reporter hide the item from their own results immediately while Rekky reviews it. Avoid sending the entire private Library, retained transcripts or raw voice to a review queue.

Create a restricted, auditable review workflow with a named operational owner, response targets and an escalation path established before pilot. Triage privacy-sensitive contact reports promptly; temporarily suppress an implicated contact or item from network retrieval when credible risk warrants it, without erasing the author's private draft or inventing a finding. Reviewers can request correction, remove the contact, restrict network visibility or remove abusive content/account access, with proportionate notices and a way to contest factual mistakes. Item/source revisions invalidate Ask indexes, cached answers, kept references and action links. Report spam and malicious reporting are rate-limited. Blocking ends the relationship and access in both directions, but cannot retract information someone already saw or copied. The exact moderation policy and response targets are pilot decisions that require an actual staffed process, not just UI buttons.

Account settings provide a plainly labelled **Delete account and data** path with identity recheck and an explicit final confirmation. This requires a successful server acknowledgement: on server commit revoke sessions and friend access, stop processing and invitations, and durably delete that new-product account's items, retained transcripts and source text, provider contacts, media still pending, indexes, cached answers, kept references and associated data. Offline, explain that account deletion requires reconnecting and do not show completion. Use idempotent deletion jobs, retries, a completion receipt and the cleanup limits in section 8; do not present physical cleanup as instantaneous. Deleting a recommender's account removes their contributions from friends' search and kept views. This never affects the old Rekky account or data. Provide a web request path for account deletion for Google Play distribution and verify the final policy, privacy notice and store disclosures before release. [Apple account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app), [Google Play account deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111)

“Find people” is a targeted lookup, not a public directory feed or a browseable popularity list. Before friendship, a discoverable result shows only the member's chosen display name, unique handle and optional photo—enough to disambiguate whom to request. Do not reveal their phone/email, city, friends list, recommendation count, item previews or activity. The connection request shows who is asking and the same brief visibility consequence, with Accept, Not now and Block. A profile that opted out of discovery is not exposed by search or contact matching. Denied permission or no match leads to the handle/invite-link path without a dead end.

Google or Apple sign-in may simplify account creation, but sign-in supplies identity rather than a portable friend graph. [Google sign-in's default profile access](https://developers.google.com/identity/sign-in/web/people) is separate from [Google People API contact scopes](https://developers.google.com/people/api/rest/v1/people.connections/list); importing Google Contacts is not the default V1 path. Broad social-network friend import is not a dependable bootstrap: [Meta describes friend-list access limits](https://about.fb.com/news/2018/12/response-to-six4three-documents/). Prefer platform contact-selection UI where available, with [iOS limited-contact access](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/contacts/accessing-the-contact-store) and [Android contact picking](https://developer.android.com/identity/providers/contacts-provider) assessed in the native spike. The old Rekky social graph does not carry over: even a former mutual follow starts as no connection in the new product.

### Contact discovery identifiers

Phone verification is optional and only needed to make an account discoverable by that number; it is not required for signup, capture or handle/invite-link use. Offer an explicit Add a discoverable phone number step, verify possession with a short-lived one-time code, rate-limit sends/attempts and explain discovery before enabling it. Never infer a verified phone from a display name, device contact or Google/Apple login. Verified email matching is allowed only for an issuer-verified or independently verified address that the member explicitly enabled for discovery; Apple relay addresses are not assumed to match an address-book email.

Normalize selected identifiers consistently, require a region where a phone number is ambiguous, and match only opted-in verified new-product accounts. A number change revokes its old match before exposing the new verified identifier; removal or discoverability opt-out removes the lookup projection. Unverified, unmatched, undiscoverable and blocked cases use the same non-enumerating response and offer an invite link. Discard nonmatching selected-contact values after the request; do not log them or build a nonmember graph. Delivery/verification provider choice and spend caps are F-03 setup work. Both one-contact selection and invite links remain required for V1; handle/link fallback works without contact permission or a discoverable phone.

## 4. Organization that does not become work

### Stable shelves and flexible retrieval contexts

Use a small stable browsing taxonomy based primarily on **what the saved subject is**, not every task it can perform. Only populated shelves appear; names are proposed copy for usability testing:

| Primary shelf | Example subjects | Useful views or facets, not more homes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Places | Restaurant branches, cafés, shops, stays, destinations | City/locality, dining, shopping, travel, cuisine, supported occasion |
| People & services | Tailors, tutors, carpenters, housemaids, coaches, individual or public service providers | Specific job/capability, supported service area, contact/referrer route |
| Things | Products, books, films, apps, sites, tools, courses as named resources | Use case, medium, brand/model, creator, language |
| Activities & events | A specific class, tour, experience or dated event rather than its provider | Activity, organizer, venue/online format, sourced date |
| Ideas & tips | Advice whose subject is not a place, person, thing or event | Topic and faithful advice; always searchable |

A coach as a person belongs in People & services; a particular swimming class belongs in Activities & events. A shop as a destination belongs in Places; a product it sells belongs in Things. Ambiguous subjects keep a broad provisional home and can be corrected. Health-related professional experiences remain attributed service knowledge, not generalized medical advice.

### Membership rules

**Specific categories (2026-09-26):** broad shelves are not the individual card label. Use stable canonical concept IDs for specific types, preferred display labels, domain-reviewed synonyms, broader/narrower relationships and compatible entity kinds. Store independent attributes such as cuisine separately; Restaurant + Italian cuisine can display as Italian restaurant. General doctor is narrower than Doctor, and a generic doctor mention does not establish a specialty. The existing understanding call selects evidence-backed IDs; the server supplies labels and dimensions. Preserve unfamiliar source-backed type/capability descriptions without creating global categories during extraction. Vocabulary changes are versioned and reviewed, while user corrections take precedence. Search shares the same concepts and ancestor/synonym projections. [Implementation and limits](./CATEGORIZATION.md)

1. Give each item one stable primary shelf based on its subject. Its capabilities, use cases, cuisine, locality and personal projects are searchable facets or views, not competing homes. A tailor praised for denim repair remains one People & services item when someone also searches for alterations.
2. Suggest the shelf and evidence-backed facets during the existing capture-wide understanding call; do not run a separate classifier for each collection or render. User moves and corrections outrank regenerated labels. A high-end restaurant belongs in Places; “special occasion” is a facet only when supported, not inferred from price.
3. A personal collection such as “Goa trip” or an AI-proposed view such as “Bengaluru places” can reference items across shelves without copying them. These lenses never change the item's primary shelf, audience, attribution or correction/contact history.
4. Automatic contextual collections use a controlled vocabulary initially. Propose a visible collection only when at least three supported items across two captures make it useful; treat this threshold as a pilot hypothesis. Users may create or pin a collection with any number of items. Personal collection names are not regenerated; taxonomy updates are versioned and overrides, pinning and exclusions persist.
5. Unknown material becomes a concise, faithful text memory in Recently added and Ideas & tips when possible. If speech cannot be processed, show a clear pending/recovery state during the bounded recovery window; do not silently claim it is saved knowledge or keep its audio indefinitely.
6. Repeated captures of the same entity can add separate experiences. Never merge different people's advice or same-name providers solely by name. Suggest a duplicate when uncertain; preserve distinct observations and Undo for any merge.

Show one level of shelf browsing with relevant filters, not a deep folder tree. At most two especially useful facet shortcuts appear initially; other filters are available on demand. Hide filters with no meaningful choices. Keep a deterministic fallback when AI is off.

### Presentation by category

**Expanded reading view (2026-09-26):** opening an item immediately presents its saved content in a warm, readable sheet with an explicit close control. Keep a compact audience selector and an overflow menu above the subject. Place the category, supported locality and eligible Maps search action together, before the attributed summary. Show caveats and practical suggestions directly; group repeated observations under one heading and keep supporting praise under “More from your note.” Omit category attributes already expressed in the label and duplicate primary-location fields. Do not use speculative semantic matching to discard distinct claims or rewrite a personal experience into an objective endorsement. The original note is a separate private disclosure, fetched only on demand with local loading/retry; deleting it is available inside that disclosure and explains shared-source effects. Review flags remain visible with a route to compare the source, without inventing a field-level diagnosis or claiming correction is implemented. Audience changes show the last acknowledged value until the server succeeds.


Default item previews show a subject, one useful piece of advice/context and attribution where relevant. Expand for source, qualifications, edits and other fields. Choose the useful action according to the category:

- Restaurant: locality, the specific tip, a supported destination or branch choice.
- Plumber: work performed, attributed experience, direct contact or a clearly labelled introduction route.
- Coach: activity, stated learner context/location, contact or booking destination.
- Product: distinguishing model/variant and why it was recommended; exact product or labelled search destination.
- Media: exact work/episode and the personal reason to keep it.
- General knowledge: the useful advice itself; no forced destination.

These are optional presentation slots, not required data-entry fields or a fixed extraction schema. Omit unknown values. A source's negative or mixed experience must remain apparent: identity-only Library cards carry a concise Caution signal, and opening them shows the complete caveat. Any preview that includes an evaluative claim (such as an Ask answer) must include the caveat that changes that claim. A card is one view of the saved knowledge, not the whole stored representation; Ask may surface different observations for a different need.

### Flexible, evidence-backed recommendation shape

Use a **small stable spine** for every saved item: stable item/owner identity, subject and entity kind, attribution, firsthand/secondhand/interest status, one primary shelf, specific supported capability/use-case phrases, typed location roles where relevant, optional matched external identity, and revision/access state. The same underlying facts support browsing and Ask; the shelf is not the search index. Keep explicit user values such as a spoken 4/5 rating as typed facts when present. AI may infer a qualitative stance for ranking if it is clearly labelled as an interpretation, but must not display an unspoken numeric score as the person's rating. Do not require ratings, pros/cons, group size, cuisine or any other category-specific field from every item.

At capture time, extract **what need the item actually serves** as evidence-backed phrases rather than a fixed exhaustive service taxonomy: “build a dining table,” “repair torn jeans,” “daily house cleaning,” or “authentic Japanese ramen.” Normalize known synonyms for indexed retrieval and allow semantic matching for unfamiliar wording, but retain the person's original claim and qualifiers. A tailor recommended for denim repair must not be presented as proven for every tailoring task. For a query, first enforce audience and supported location scope, then match the specific need and rank by personal evidence, source and relevance; never let a broad category or embedding similarity manufacture a capability.

Location has distinct roles: a physical venue's actual branch/city; a provider's **stated or confirmed service area**; the location of a past job or experience; a speaker's comparison or contextual mention; and an optional approximate capture-location hint. These roles are stored separately with evidence and uncertainty. A job completed in Indiranagar is not proof that the carpenter serves all Bengaluru. A remote tutor is location-independent only when that mode is actually supported. Unknown service area stays unknown and may warrant a later follow-up; it is not silently converted to the user's current city.

Alongside that spine, store an **open-ended set of concise observations** selected from what this particular person actually said. Each observation has a readable claim, whether it is praise, warning, suitability, comparison or another useful meaning, any important qualifier or scope, its owner/source, and support from the temporary input at extraction time. It may have optional normalized facets for retrieval, but an unfamiliar observation must remain saveable even without a known facet. Observations are not arbitrary new database columns or model-written UI code. Stable contracts carry the observation list; indexed projections and category-specific components can evolve without discarding the underlying meaning. User-authored corrections outrank regenerated observations.

For the Kuuraku example, the spine records a firsthand restaurant recommendation in Gurugram, an explicit 4/5 rating, and a separately sourced place match if one is supported. Flexible observations retain the user's ramen comparison, helpful/friendly staff, suitability for roughly 3–4 people and the small-table/large-group warning. “Authentic” remains the person's opinion; “best ramen I've tried in Delhi” is a comparison, not an address. A strict Delhi-city search must not turn the Gurugram venue into a Delhi result; a Delhi-NCR search can include it when its actual location qualifies.

Preserve a **short, faithful account** of the user's useful meaning as a backstop when a claim does not map neatly to a typed facet. Check that each material assertion or caveat in the transcript is represented by an observation or that account; repetitions, fillers and false starts may be dropped. If meaningful content is unaccounted for, retry within budget or mark the capture partial. Retained owner-only transcripts support inspection, correction and explicitly requested re-extraction after audio deletion. They do not prove the transcriber heard correctly and are not a substitute for faithful extraction. Label them as machine transcripts, retain source/pipeline revision metadata, and evaluate against labelled audio for transcription errors as well as against transcripts for extraction errors. Network evidence is the authorized item's supported account/observations, never access to its full source transcript.

## 5. The reference journeys

### A. One messy recording, several organized memories

Input: “Try the ramen at Kuuraku in Indiranagar. Raju fixed our kitchen tap really well—Priya has his number. Meera teaches swimming near our apartment; I haven't tried her classes.”

1. Tap Remember, speak, tap Done. Safely queue a temporary recording before acknowledging “Processing your thought”; the user can leave immediately.
2. Show a small, dismissible processing receipt. Once a usable transcript is durably committed, queue audio deletion; extraction continues from the retained private text. Only after useful items commit does the receipt say knowledge is saved. Never add processed-audio playback.
3. As understanding completes, the receipt can become “Kept your ramen spot, plumber and swimming tip.” Shelf references make the destination of each item clear without opening three cards.
4. Kuuraku appears in Places; Raju and Meera in People & services, with Meera explicitly marked as untried. Their different jobs and places remain distinct search facets. “Near our apartment” does not become a public address or a confirmed service area.
5. Rekky first uses supported context and bounded factual lookup to check Kuuraku's identity. If the branch remains ambiguous, it offers a small candidate choice; it does not ask for a location field by default.
6. Offer at most one useful, optional completion while the receipt is active. For example, “Add a contact for Raju?” A direct provider route can open the phone's native contact picker after permission, with manual number/email/link entry if denied or unavailable. A missing contact never blocks saving. If the useful detail is discovered late or the receipt was dismissed, keep the action on Raju's item without reopening UI; an unanswered completion may become the single quiet suggestion on a later visit under the follow-up rules in section 6.
7. Contacts are deliberate and item-scoped. Attaching Priya as a referrer produces “Ask Priya about Raju,” never “Call Raju.” Never treat a spoken or transcribed number as actionable until confirmed.
8. Dismissal survives unchanged-input retries. Late results update the library without opening UI or stealing focus.

The same completion pattern applies to an English tutor: save the user's experience first, recognize that a direct contact would make it actionable, then offer “Add contact” with the native picker and a manual fallback. The app must not demand a phone number before saving, attach a plausible same-name contact automatically, or imply that a number found on the public web belongs to this private individual.

### B. Recover a useful memory

“Who fixed our kitchen tap?” returns Raju, the remembered work and a contact route. “Where should we get ramen around Indiranagar?” returns relevant saved/shared experiences, with attribution and a useful destination.

For broad requests, group results by the intent that makes them belong together. “Help me settle into this neighbourhood” can legitimately mix home services, activities and dining, with sections explaining that context. Ordinary collection browsing never defaults to this mixture.

### C. Network knowledge and directed sharing

After a user has signed in and accepted the account visibility explanation, each successfully distilled, completed item defaults to **Friends**, available to reciprocal Rekky friends automatically. The only item audiences in V1 are **Friends** and **Private**. There is no per-recommendation audience picker or mandatory review before publication. Show the effective audience during capture; Private is available before Done, from the receipt, or later in item detail. A Private choice included in a new upload or already acknowledged by the server is applied before any friend-visible commit. If an upload already reached the server and a subsequent Private change is still offline, the server may finish under its last acknowledged audience; show Privacy change pending, with the previous network audience, until acknowledgement. Do not promise retroactive prevention of views. New friends can see existing Friends items; ending the friendship removes access on server commit. Private items stay in the owner's Library and Ask. Pending/failed material, retained transcripts, source text, raw audio, approximate capture-location context, uncertain contact tokens and internal agent notes are never network content. Friends-visible never means public-web publication.

If the user deliberately attaches a confirmed provider contact to a Friends item, friends can use it without a separate contact-visibility toggle. Explain this on attachment and offer Private for mistakes; a pending Private change must be acknowledged before the new contact is published. A referrer's private contact remains excluded. Selecting one phone contact never imports the rest of the address book. Private/Delete and friend removal revoke access on server commit and invalidate search results, kept references and cached previews. Their offline pending state is explicit. Delete also removes the owner's item and associated source content under section 8; confirm shared-transcript effects before committing. Neither operation can retract information already seen or externally copied.

An optional directed Share/Reply action can still send a selected item in response to a specific person without rewriting it; this is a convenience, not what makes the item available to friends. It never includes sibling items or raw processing material.

### D. Save from another app

Text and URLs enter through the platform share surface and use the same durable capture service. Aim for save-and-return, but prove the actual native behavior before promising a compact extension. Unsupported media receives an honest explanation. Start native share feasibility work early because opening Rekky every time is a major adoption cost.

### E. Make a correction

The item menu offers subject, advice, organization and destination correction. A short voice correction belongs to one item/field, shows its interpreted proposal, and requires Apply with Undo. The owner can open **Transcript** from the capture/item detail, clearly labelled machine-generated and private, correct its text or delete it separately. Store the current source revision and metadata, label user-corrected text accordingly, and do not accumulate undisclosed full-text history. An explicit Reprocess action, with current processing permission and budget, proposes changes against that source revision. User corrections, contacts, Private choices and deleted-item tombstones outrank regenerated output; ambiguous item reassociation needs a choice. For completed captures, preview additions/changes before Apply, and do not republish deleted items or broaden audiences. Do not rerun old transcripts simply because a model changes. A voice correction uses temporary audio and a private text transcript under the same policy; deleting its source leaves an applied correction intact.

## 6. The work delegated to AI

| Work | Responsibility and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Understand | Transcribe permitted voice; preserve English/Hindi/Hinglish, uncertainty, corrections and spoken contact tokens |
| Extract | Separate subjects and a context-specific set of useful observations with source evidence; preserve a concise faithful account and check coverage of material claims/caveats; support general knowledge and overflow without silent truncation |
| Organize | Suggest one stable subject-based shelf plus evidence-backed subtype, capability/use-case and location-role facets; user corrections have precedence |
| Connect | Link supported entities and related memories while retaining independent observations and attribution |
| Complete | An item-scoped agent identifies a useful gap, chooses an allowed tool or follow-up, evaluates the returned evidence and stops when further work adds little value; use minimal identity clues and bounded requests |
| Retrieve | Route by question type; enforce access and supported location scope; retrieve exact and meaning-based candidates from own/authorized network knowledge as justified by evaluation; rerank against actual evidence before answering; offer separately labelled public discovery on sparse results when enabled |
| Answer | Construct concise claims from accessible evidence, preserving disagreement and uncertainty |
| Prepare action | Choose an appropriate component or draft a share/reply; application code validates action, destination and audience |

Audit and adapt the legacy transcription and Gemini extraction integrations as initial candidates; verify they satisfy this product's explicit provider permissions, transcript retention and budget rules. Benchmark cheaper transcription on Hindi/Hinglish, local names, spoken numbers and noise before switching the initial default. Jev stays available behind an adapter but is off the normal creation path unless evaluation demonstrates incremental value. Quality, latency and total cost govern model selection; legacy deployment status does not authorize a provider in the new product.

Prefer **one capture-level understanding call with independent completion state per item** for normal bounded captures. The path is transcription into a durably retained private text source, understanding, then only useful tool work. Audio deletion does not wait for extraction or enrichment once transcription is accepted. Understanding extracts subjects, observations, faithful accounts, attribution and organization and proposes each item's first action. Category labels do not select a mandatory research recipe. Persisted completion state does not imply a fresh planner call. Supplied identifiers and permitted confirmed mappings can remove further research. Places/services receive deeper evaluation first; every category stays savable without enrichment. One-call completion is a measured efficiency target, not a correctness gate: bounded segmentation or additional checking is permitted when labelled results justify it.

Treat every capture and tool result as untrusted content, not instructions to change permissions or execute actions. Validate types/lengths, attribution, contact roles and cited source spans in application code. Where feasible, replace literal phone/email values in extraction input with source-mapped opaque tokens and restore only values present in that source; a model cannot invent a usable contact. Spoken contact values necessarily reach the consented transcriber, remain unconfirmed after transcription and never become callable until deliberately confirmed. Selected contact snapshots are not sent to later model reasoning. Keep raw source content, contacts, tokens and signed asset URLs out of logs/analytics/crash reports; record opaque IDs, stage/error codes, revisions, timing and costs. Encrypt source/knowledge storage in transit and at rest and enforce owner authorization on every source read; cloud processing is not described as end-to-end encrypted. Audit actual provider retention/training settings before enabling each adapter.

### Default processing path and selective continuation

1. Transcribe under the explicit processing permission in section 8. Commit an accepted transcript with owner, capture and source revision before queuing audio deletion. Retry genuinely failed or unusable transcription only while audio remains within its window; never retranscribe just because understanding is retried. After audio deletion, correction/re-extraction uses the saved text, and the UI does not offer audio recovery.
2. Request a structured understanding result for the whole bounded capture, including evidence references and each item's first proposed `use_tool`, `propose_follow_up` or `finish` decision. Resolve proposals to stable item IDs when committing. Validate the knowledge payload separately from the optional action payload: a malformed action must not invalidate otherwise valid saved advice. Processing overflow remains explicit and bounded, with no silent truncation.
3. Perform source/evidence and material-claim coverage checks against the private transcript, commit useful items and checkpoint initial decisions. The normal path avoids redundant classification, summary, organization, planner and reviewer calls; measured harder cases may use a bounded fallback. A useful item can finish or persist a contact question without research. Known partial/failed extraction stays owner-only until resolved; explicitly mark coverage status and never turn syntactically valid JSON into a completeness guarantee.
4. Execute independent, authorized tool actions concurrently within capture budgets and provider limits. Commit a result directly only when application checks support its identity and evidence; otherwise retain a suggestion or continue reasoning. The UI shows saved knowledge before optional enrichment finishes.
5. Make an additional item-scoped model call only for a useful unresolved decision: interpreting selected page evidence, comparing conflicting candidates, or choosing a justified fallback after a failed lookup. Use only the item's compact evidence and tool results. A missing private contact normally waits for the user. An ambiguity caused by missing user knowledge is not solved by repeatedly buying more reasoning.

Compare this combined path against the separated baseline on held-out examples before enablement; saving calls must not reduce preservation of caveats, item/contact association or identity precision. If long or complex captures need a different processing path, use measured complexity/failure signals and preserve explicit partial status. Output budgets should be compact but sufficient for the useful observations; do not optimize token count by deleting meaning. See the published rationale for combining related requests in [OpenAI's latency guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latency-optimization).

Coverage checks gate claiming complete knowledge extraction and network publication, rather than retention of already transcribed audio. Same-model self-review is not an independent completeness guarantee; source spans can detect unsupported text but not every omission or transcription error. Evaluate audio → transcript → saved knowledge → later answer against human-labelled examples. Use bounded deeper review for conflicts, unsupported fields or partial processing and test escalation signals against measured error reduction. Retained transcripts are available for owner recovery; ordinary production transcripts/audio never become evaluation data without separate explicit consent.

Use two distinct external-search purposes. **Factual completion** attaches sourced identity or destination information to an existing user/network item. **Public discovery** may offer new options when personal/network coverage is insufficient and the user chooses to look wider. Do not merge their provenance or ranking into a single undifferentiated recommendation pool. A public place page can support an identity or destination; the experience claim still belongs to the original person. A public service listing is a lead, not a verified personal recommendation. Do not infer that a same-name professional in a public result is the person someone recommended.

Retain automatic external-request limits of two per item and five per capture. The $0.10-per-capture completion allowance covers continuation model calls and external tools; it is a ceiling, not a spending target. Charge the combined understanding call once to the initial-understanding budget, never again for each item's first decision. Explicit retry/refresh actions allow two external requests and $0.05 across model and tool calls; the completion pilot ceiling remains $10/day. These are policy limits, not measured current provider costs. Reserve configured paid-rate estimates before dispatch, including model output-token bounds, retries and fallbacks; reconcile actual usage without releasing uncertain spend after a crash. Transcription, initial understanding and Ask generation need separate configured caps before pilot. Track total creation cost across transcription, understanding, reviews, completion and retries, with an all-stage account/project ceiling. No agent or provider fallback may create its own fresh budget.

Current hours can be requested for supported places; other volatile details open the source initially. Provider retention permissions apply to extracted snippets and metadata as well as full results. No repeated automatic research on old libraries.

**Initial useful-link checkpoint (2026-09-26):** provide a **Search in Maps** action in a place's detail when its saved representation includes a name and one distinct, explicitly stated venue locality. Build the Google Maps search URL in application code from those two fields only; no generated URL, transcript, opinion, referrer, contact, inferred device location or extra model call. Merely viewing or saving the item makes no external request. The user's tap opens Maps or its web fallback, with a recoverable launch failure. A search handoff is not an attached or verified listing. Suppress it for conflicting venue locations and non-place items; practice/service-area/past-experience/context locations do not become venue addresses. This gives existing saved places an immediate action without reprocessing their sources. [Google Maps URLs](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started)

Exact **Open in Maps** destinations remain the subsequent factual-completion step: resolve name/locality against provider evidence, retain the listing identifier and match provenance under provider storage rules, and handle ambiguous branches as candidates. Automatic identity attachment remains disabled until the held-out precision and coverage gate below passes. No lookup provider, resolver, exact-listing attachment or background research is enabled by this search-link checkpoint. Saving stays independent of optional enrichment.

### Completion agent: execution contract

The agent's objective is to make the saved recommendation understandable and actionable with the least additional work. Flexible observations remain available even when no known facet fits. A restaurant may benefit from directions, an individual tutor from a contact route, an online tutor from teaching context, and a warning from no additional work. These are evaluation examples rather than category-specific scripts. Do not optimize for field count or a universal completeness score.

1. **Load the checkpoint.** Start from the first decision produced by capture understanding. For a necessary continuation, supply the current item revision, faithful account, observations/evidence, confirmed identities and corrections, available contact-route status, relevant tool observations, dismissed questions, permissions and remaining budget. Include only this item's authorized context. Raw recordings, full transcripts, unrelated sibling items and the user's address book are unnecessary. Raw contact values do not enter the reasoning context when presence and provider/referrer role suffice.
2. **Choose an action when needed.** The initial structured understanding result, or a justified continuation response, proposes `use_tool`, `propose_follow_up` or `finish`, with the useful gap, a short user-facing justification, references to supporting observations, and typed arguments. Gaps can be described flexibly; tool names and response envelopes are validated contracts. `finish` records an honest outcome such as actionable, useful as saved, or unresolved with a reason. Do not call the model again merely to repeat a valid checkpointed decision. Model confidence alone cannot authorize an identity match.
3. **Execute and observe.** The runtime validates the action against ownership, consent, budget and tool rules, persists the reservation and invocation, executes the tool, and checkpoints the bounded result. The next decision can use that result to change direction or stop. Code may commit a fully validated result directly; another model call is unnecessary for a mechanically resolved identifier.
4. **Commit supported changes.** The model proposes additive identity/fact/action changes referring to actual evidence returned by tools. The commit validator checks referenced evidence, user corrections, contradictions, retention rights and item revisions. Uncertain identities remain suggestions. Personal experience and public facts retain distinct provenance; the agent cannot change a rating, claim public evidence validates praise, attach a private contact, or alter sharing permissions.
5. **Stop or suspend.** Stop when no useful gap remains, no authorized action can improve it, evidence remains ambiguous, the user is needed, or a limit is reached. Keep useful saved knowledge available. Waiting for a user is a persisted state with no live worker or model session. Completion uses the saved item, so raw-audio deletion never waits for enrichment or a follow-up answer.

Start with at most three decision attempts per item operation: its decision from combined understanding plus at most two additional model calls, including invalid-response retries. Add a configurable capture-wide continuation ceiling, initially four calls, so a many-item note cannot multiply reasoning indefinitely. Apply the shared financial and external-request limits as well; these are maximums, not required steps. Initial-understanding retries are separately bounded and charged once per attempt. Calls have configured input/output bounds and deadlines; public HTTP calls retain a five-second deadline covering body reads. Persist every attempted call against the same operation. Give each capture's items a first eligible tool attempt before second attempts consume its shared allowance, with bounded concurrency. Do not launch all search providers speculatively; the agent requests a capability and the adapter selects an eligible provider. Additional page reads and provider fallbacks count as requests too. Repeated normalized tool requests with unchanged evidence are rejected or reuse permitted results. Budget exhaustion yields deferred work, never a falsely complete match. These pilot limits can be adjusted from measured useful-action coverage, latency and spend.

### Tool boundary and evidence

| Tool capability | Input and permitted result |
| --- | --- |
| Read an existing identity | Owner-authorized item/entity references or identity clues; return applicable confirmed mappings and permitted cached public identity, with provenance |
| Find a place | Item-backed name, explicit locality/branch and permitted approximate location bias; return a small candidate set and source references |
| Search public sources | Relevant public identity/resource clues and the declared gap; return bounded results under the provider's retention configuration |
| Read a public page | A supplied URL or an actual tool-returned destination; return bounded structured metadata/excerpts with source/check time and retention policy |
| Propose a follow-up | Item/gap reference, concise question, expected benefit and an allowed native input type; create a proposal for later presentation, never open a UI or message someone directly |
| Submit a completion result | Evidence-linked proposals and outcome; application code validates and commits authorized changes |

The model adapter may change independently of these capabilities. Start by evaluating the existing Gemini adapter for structured tool decisions; Jev remains optional for classification and is not the completion orchestrator. Provider adapters hide credentials and translate capability requests to Google, Brave or Parallel as configured. Public-person searches require explicit evidence of a public professional identity; a personal name plus occupation is insufficient. The runtime constructs outbound queries from permitted identity clues, rejecting unrelated review text, contact data and private referrer details. A destination must come from user input or tool evidence, never a model-invented URL.

Tool results are untrusted data. The public-page reader validates public HTTP(S) destinations and every redirect, blocks private networks, limits bytes/redirects, and uses no credentials or JavaScript. Page instructions cannot change the agent's objective, permissions or tools. Persist only permitted facts/evidence: references alone do not grant retention rights to a provider's excerpts. For places, validate all available distinguishing clues, including city, branch and business type; a lone result in a truncated search is not proof of uniqueness. Existing exact-name/address-substring checks require strengthening, and uncertain spelling or branch matches require a suggestion or user choice.

### Follow-ups on later visits

Persist a proposed follow-up with its item revision, gap/evidence references, expected benefit, question, allowed input type and lifecycle: proposed, shown, snoozed, dismissed, answered or obsolete. Input types initially include candidate choice, native contact attachment, short text, supplied link and an item-scoped voice reply. A new gap can use a short-text fallback without adding a category form. Answers and selected contacts are explicit user writes with confirmation where needed, including interpreted voice proposals.

At most one proactive completion appears per capture session; on a later visit the same optional Home slot may show at most one high-value unanswered suggestion across the library, or the relevant item can offer it when opened. Never interrupt recording, Ask input or an active answer. Receipt dismissal only closes the receipt. An explicitly dismissed question stays dismissed for unchanged evidence; snooze obeys its recorded time. Simply opening the app does not trigger new research or resurrect ignored questions. Revalidate usefulness, permissions, item revision and whether the gap is already filled before display; choose the eligible suggestion from stored benefit/relevance signals without a new model call on login. No push notification or completion inbox is required for V1.

Store presentation claims and follow-up mutations in the owner change feed so devices do not independently show or answer the same question. The user's reply is durable locally, shows pending synchronization where needed, and resumes only the affected item after server acknowledgement when additional agent work is useful. Answering a question does not silently grant external-lookup consent or a fresh budget: use the remaining capture allowance; a separate explicit retry can receive the action allowance. Stale replies require narrow reconciliation, and resolved/deleted items obsolete their questions.

### Ask retrieval architecture

Rekky searches **short, attributed experiences and observations**, not arbitrary chunks of long documents. One generic vector-RAG pipeline is insufficient: similarity can surface the wrong city, a person with a related but unproven skill, or a result the asker cannot access. The initial structured/lexical path remains useful for exact recall and offline own-data search; add semantic retrieval and reranking where held-out questions show measurable improvement. The target is query-aware hybrid retrieval, not an agent or extra model call for every Ask. [Microsoft's hybrid-search documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/search/search-get-started-vector) describes the lexical/vector/reranking pattern; its results are background evidence, not a promised Rekky gain.

Personal recall and new recommendations use different location behavior. “Who fixed our kitchen tap?” should retrieve the remembered person even when their current service area is unknown or the asker has moved; do not inject the default city as a hard constraint into a non-geographic recall question. Show the recorded experience location when known. “Who can fix a tap in Whitefield?” asks for current coverage: distinguish supported matches from useful candidates whose availability/area needs checking. This distinction is part of F-01 evaluation, not deferred to advanced search.

Route a question into an explicit query plan, retaining the original wording and supported conversation context:

| Query class | Example | Preferred first path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact recall | “Who was the plumber Priya told me about?” | Names, attribution, relationship and lexical/structured search; no semantic or generation call when an exact supported result and native action suffice |
| Constrained recommendation | “A carpenter who can build a dining table in Bengaluru” | Access and service-area constraints, capability matches, then lexical plus semantic candidate retrieval if needed |
| Quality or suitability | “A quiet Japanese place for three” | Place/city filter plus observation-level retrieval for atmosphere, cuisine, group suitability and caveats |
| Comparison | “Which ramen places did my friends like most?” | Group supported experiences by confirmed entity without merging authors, explicit ratings or attributed stance, then compare with disagreement visible |
| Broad or multi-part | “Help me settle into Bengaluru” | Bounded decomposition into a few intents/shelves, each using the same authorized retrieval; synthesize only after evidence is collected |

Query understanding produces requested task, entity/person clues, qualities, hard versus soft constraints, location scope, source scope and whether the question is a follow-up. An explicit place or “near me” request is resolved using the location policy above; ambiguous city, task or referent can trigger one targeted clarification rather than a guessed filter. Source authorization is checked **before** retrieval and again before presentation or action. Private contacts/referrer names are not sent to external search or to a broader network index. Network counts, candidate previews, caches and embeddings must not leak revoked or unshared material.

Index each stable item and its individual useful observations as linked projections. An observation's searchable text includes the subject and necessary item context, so “small tables” never becomes an orphaned restaurant result. Store the supported task/capability, negation and caveat, typed location role, author, source, item/evidence IDs and revision alongside it; do not invent missing tags or use the shelf as the only search key. Preserve separate experiences by different people even when they point to one confirmed entity. This applies the useful idea behind [contextual retrieval](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/contextual-retrieval) to Rekky's short records without generating extra prose for every indexed fragment.

Generate candidates with structured/lexical search and, where evaluation warrants, a multilingual semantic index over the same authorized scope. Fuse small candidate sets and deduplicate by item/evidence, not by common person name; group confirmed shared entities only after retrieval. Hard constraints such as access, actual venue city, supported service area and explicit exclusions are enforced as application rules, never traded against a high vector score. For a small authorized set, exact filtered search may be faster and higher-recall than approximate nearest-neighbor search. When scale requires approximate indexes, benchmark filtered recall: [pgvector documents](https://github.com/pgvector/pgvector) that ANN filtering can otherwise return too few qualifying rows. Keep the read path in Postgres initially; add a separate search service only if measured scale or quality requires it.

Rerank bounded candidate windows against the **specific supporting claims**, weighing task fit, location certainty, firsthand/secondhand status, caveats, explicit ratings where relevant, source relationship or user-selected trust, and actionability. Continue retrieval/ranking through subsequent windows so every found, authorized result above the evidence/relevance bar remains reachable; a bounded model context is not a display cutoff. Page the validated result set with a stable cursor and preserve ranking across pages. Do not claim an exact total when the index or retrieval pass cannot establish one. Do not substitute popularity for fit or invent a trust score. Use deterministic checks for hard constraints and a tested reranker or small model for nuanced evidence fit; tune weights and abstention on Rekky-labelled questions. Jev can be benchmarked as an optional fast [typed Choice/Score judge](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction) for query routing or atomic candidate judgments, possibly scoring several dimensions in one call. It is not a retriever, source verifier or answer writer. Its reported confidence is not measured correctness; compare Jev with a dedicated multilingual reranker and a no-reranker baseline for relevance, calibration, latency and total cost before enabling it on Ask. Late-interaction retrieval or GraphRAG are later experiments only if simpler methods miss valuable queries; neither is a default dependency for short personal recommendations. [GraphRAG's original work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16130) targets global sensemaking over large corpora rather than an ordinary person/place lookup.

Only supported results enter answer composition; optional synthesis summarizes a bounded leading set without replacing the full validated result list. A simple exact answer can render directly from validated fields; richer comparisons use a bounded synthesis call with claim-to-evidence references. Verify every displayed claim, source label, location assertion and allowed action against current accessible records. If reranking or synthesis fails, show the useful retrieved items without fabricated explanation. If no result clears the evidence bar, say what is missing and offer a precise clarification, an optional wider-scope view or separately consented public discovery. Do not convert an inferred qualitative opinion into the author's numeric rating.

Set separate Ask time, token and spend budgets before the pilot. The normal exact path uses no paid query-planning/reranking/synthesis call; the hybrid path permits at most one query embedding, one bounded shortlist judgment and one synthesis call when the answer needs it. A broad question may decompose into a small configured number of subqueries under the same request budget and deadline, never an unbounded agent loop. Reuse permitted projections and coalesce equivalent work; cache only with audience/grant/revision keys and invalidate on corrections or revocation. Return validated candidates promptly while optional synthesis completes. When a provider is slow or the budget is exhausted, degrade to grounded native result cards or an honest partial answer rather than searching forever.

### Answers and adaptive UI

Define a versioned answer contract containing claim text, supporting entry/evidence IDs, source type (`own`, `network`, `public`), uncertainty, result scope, stable result/group IDs, page cursor and allowed action references. The client assembles a small vocabulary of polished native components selected for the user's need: concise answer, service with contact/referrer route, place with directions, evidence-backed comparison, public lead, candidate choice and share preview. A single qualifying result can lead with one focused card; several lead with a richer first preview and compact, scannable results below; many load in pages without an arbitrary visible-result cap. Group recommendations about one **confirmed** place or provider into one result while preserving each person's separate experience, caveats and attribution. Never merge two providers merely because their names match. Useful but unknown-area experiences appear prominently in the separate Worth checking section with the missing fact stated; unrelated or genuinely weak matches may remain under a Possible leads expansion. Neither is labelled as confirmed local coverage. The model does not produce arbitrary Flutter code or executable destinations. Each component makes the next useful action obvious without a wall of explanation.

Retrieve authorized evidence before synthesis. Check source revisions/access again before returning an answer and before executing its action. Stream status or already validated results; do not display unsupported generated claims as if settled. If synthesis fails, retrieved items remain usable. If evidence is missing, say what is unknown and offer a specific next step.

For a new location-dependent recommendation, an explicit city/area wins. Otherwise display a user-selected city or, only with permission, an approximate city suggestion; never treat the asker's location as evidence about a saved provider. Confident local-service matches require supported coverage. If strong remembered experience exists but service area is unknown, show a visible **Worth checking — service area unconfirmed** section directly after confirmed results; when no confirmed result exists, show that useful section immediately rather than a false empty state. Its contact/check-availability action cannot claim coverage. Known contradictory areas stay excluded from confident results. Personal recall does not acquire a hard city constraint unless the question requests it. Strict venue-city requests still use actual branch/city, with named regions such as Delhi-NCR handled explicitly. Remote results remain distinct. Location scope can be changed or cleared without re-recording.

Public discovery has a distinct consent, cost and provenance path from enrichment of an existing saved item. Sanitize queries so private contacts, referrer names and unrelated note text are not sent to public search. A public result can be kept, but remains labelled as a public lead until the user contributes an actual experience. Do not let a public result inherit network trust or appear in a share as someone's recommendation.

## 7. Visual and interaction specification

The next design is built and reviewed in Flutter using realistic sample content, then connected incrementally to real behavior. Time-box the initial two-direction exploration to three working days once tooling runs: compare Ask/Remember, a recording receipt, a shelf and a multi-result answer on phones, choose a direction, and extend it during live slices. This is a planning allocation, not a promised completion date. A polished simulation of every future screen is not a prerequisite to F-01. A standalone browser simulation is not a substitute for the native interaction review.

### Creative brief and visual system

**Warm and distinctive; soft, but exact.** Rekky should feel like a trusted personal object, not a productivity dashboard, social feed, generic chatbot or playful toy. Warmth comes from human typography, generous but efficient spacing, tactile feedback and the prominence of real people's advice. Exactness comes from clear information hierarchy, source/uncertainty labels, precise actions and dependable native controls. Distinctiveness must be earned by the composition and behavior of these surfaces, not by gradients, decorative AI imagery, excessive copy or an animated assistant persona. The older monochrome browser prototype is not the visual target.

In F-00, explore at least two materially different *native* visual directions against the same realistic journeys, then select and document one. A candidate direction uses warm paper-like neutrals, deep readable ink, one recognizable warm action accent and subtle, consistent shelf cues; this is a hypothesis, **not a locked palette**. Choose typography with a distinct display voice and a highly legible reading face that supports Hindi and English gracefully. Define light/dark tokens, spacing, radii, icon language, imagery rules and motion/haptic tokens together; avoid arbitrary one-off styles as new screens appear. Do not import web-style navigation chrome or insist that iOS and Android look pixel-identical. Preserve their expected system sheets, gestures, permission dialogs and accessibility behavior. Apple distinguishes top-level tab navigation from actions; Remember therefore remains a prominent action, not a fake third tab. [Apple tab-bar guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars), [Apple design principles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles)

### Screen composition and interaction character

- **Ask home:** a calm, immediately usable question entry and a clearly separate, thumb-reachable Remember action. No empty chat transcript, rotating prompt carousel, content feed or multi-panel dashboard. One optional grounded return-to-knowledge or completion cue may occupy a stable slot; it disappears when it has no value. The user can tell within a glance whether they are asking Rekky or giving it something to remember.
- **Recording:** a focused, nearly full-screen state that starts on the Remember tap, with clear Done/Discard, elapsed time and truthful input activity. Keep controls reachable one-handed; never rely on press-and-hold alone, an ornamental waveform or haptics alone. Finishing feels decisive: the user receives an immediate durable-queue acknowledgement and can leave, with no forced review form or blocking processing animation.
- **Saved receipt and follow-up:** show a short, honest status and the distilled subjects as they become available. A place with a supported destination can expose Directions; a service missing a critical contact can later expose one small Attach contact action. Do not reopen a sheet on delayed completion, ask several questions at once, or make a user read internal AI steps. Privacy can be changed from the receipt. Processing, failure and retry states are visually distinct but not alarming.
- **Library:** stable, recognizable shelves rather than one mixed card grid. Places should privilege name, locality and a real destination; People & services should privilege capability, supported area, recommender and contact route; Things and Ideas can use quieter, text-led previews. A subject appears once in its primary shelf. Space, typography and object-specific action placement do the organizing; category color is only a secondary cue. Your Library and From your network have visibly different scopes, and All locations remains easy to reach.
- **Ask answer:** treat the response as an assembled native result surface, not a paragraph from a chatbot. The query, effective city/scope and source boundary are clear. Show a short direct takeaway where evidence supports it, then every qualifying result in a scannable ranked list with the useful differentiator, attribution, caveat and one primary action. One rich lead may anchor the page, but never hides other relevant options. Services foreground contact, places Directions, comparisons the criteria, and gaps an honest next step. Public leads and uncertain possibilities live in distinct labelled areas; no fabricated image or badge upgrades weak evidence.
- **Detail and correction:** let someone understand exactly what was saved, who said it, what was externally resolved and what remains uncertain. Preserve the person's voice in a concise faithful account, not a raw transcript. Write summaries as useful saved notes rather than repeatedly saying “You recommend”, “You strongly recommend” or restating the subject to announce an endorsement. Show firsthand attribution separately; preserve named referrers, secondhand/untried status, uncertainty, personal comparisons and negative/mixed stance in the prose wherever necessary. Opinions must not become verified public facts. Place a relevant destination action near the summary. Put correction, Private/Delete and source detail behind clear, accessible controls without forcing those controls into every preview.

Motion is restrained and state-driven: a recording starts, a save settles, a result becomes available. Use subtle haptics where helpful, with visible and screen-reader-equivalent feedback; never make motion or haptics the only signal. Prefer immediate, quiet success and reversible actions over celebratory confetti or repeated toasts. UI copy is short, human and specific (“Saved”, “Choose a place”, “Attach contact”), not anthropomorphic narration of agent steps. Real, licensed, correctly attributed imagery may improve place recognition; never generate a picture implying that it depicts a particular provider or venue. Attractive typographic fallbacks must work when imagery is absent.

The design review must compare the same empty, sparse and established states; a mixed voice note; a single restaurant; a tutor missing contact; multi-result Ask; no result; offline/interrupted capture; delayed enrichment; and privacy changes. Evaluate each direction on glance comprehension, one-handed reach, trust/source clarity, information density, delight without delay and whether a user can find a useful action without reading instructions. Keep the chosen visual system as a documented Flutter token/component specification and test it on actual iOS and Android devices before broad implementation. [Apple accessibility guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility)

### Quality floor

- Maintain a clear hierarchy per surface. On the Ask home, Ask and Remember are both immediately legible; visual emphasis may favor Remember for an empty account and Ask for an established one, but their positions do not move. Avoid competing hero panels. Within a recording or answer, the current task has one dominant action. Routine status is conveyed by the object/state rather than repeated prose, badges or instructional panels.
- Sources, editing and diagnostics are progressively disclosed. Processing consent still explains material choices when needed; minimal UI cannot mean hidden sharing or processing.
- Keep labels and navigation positions stable. Adaptive content changes within predictable component slots.
- Check 320/375/414/768 logical widths, 200% text scaling, long Hindi/English content, light/dark appearance, reduced motion, VoiceOver/TalkBack and keyboard-safe sheets. Category distinction cannot rely on color alone.

Review failure states alongside the happy path: empty/sparse library, no microphone/contacts/location permission, offline save, unusable audio, full storage, interrupted finalization, ambiguous destination, no search result, conflicting advice, revoked share and delayed processing.

## 8. Data, synchronization and access

### Core records

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Capture processing envelope | Account owner, capture time/timezone, state, audio expiry and deletion audit; temporary audio reference plus owner-only durable source-text/transcript reference and source revision |
| Retained source text/transcript | Owner-only text, machine-transcribed versus typed/shared provenance, current revision, transcription/pipeline metadata, coverage/reprocess state and deletion tombstone; never part of a Friends projection |
| Knowledge entry | Stable useful item independent of an extraction run; can contain several evidence-backed observations |
| Evidence/observation | Flexible, item-scoped claims and a concise faithful account, with attribution, firsthand/secondhand/interest status, caveats, qualifiers, optional normalized capability/use-case facets and extraction-time support; not a full transcript or a permanent audio reference |
| Entity/destination | Supported identity, match state, source/check time and provider retention policy |
| Contact route | Item-scoped provider/referrer role, explicit value and confirmation state |
| Location scope | Typed venue branch/city, provider service area, past-job location, contextual/comparison mention or approximate capture hint; evidence, uncertainty and freshness stay distinct |
| Shelf/context membership | One stable subject-based shelf plus non-owning personal/contextual view references, evidence or assignment reason, provenance and user override |
| Search projection | Rebuildable item/observation-level lexical and optional semantic index entries with subject context, capability, negation, typed location, evidence/author IDs, current friend-access policy and revision; no orphaned chunks or merged people by common name |
| Kept network reference | Viewer, source item, explicit Keep action and original attribution; contents remain subject to current reciprocal-friend access and item removal |
| Network visibility | Completed item revision, owner, `friends`/`private` choice (signed-in account default `friends`; explicit per-item/capture `private` override), pending capture-level Private choice applied atomically before publication, deletion tombstone and visibility disclosure acceptance; audience derives from current reciprocal friendships, not per-item recipient grants |
| Connection/invitation | Initiator, recipient or expiring invitation, pending/accepted/declined/removed/blocked state, verified account identifier where applicable, and access revision; contact discovery is optional and never stores a nonmember address-book graph |
| Report/review | Scoped item, contact or account reference; reporter, reason, review state and restricted audit trail; no raw voice or unrelated private-library copy |
| Account deletion operation | Identity-checked online request, access/session revocation at server commit, idempotent cleanup state and minimal completion receipt; no old-product identifier or data mutation |
| Directed share/reply | Optional selected recipient and item/action reference for a direct response; not the gate for friend access |
| Completion run/step | Item and pipeline revisions, consent generation, checkpoint, tool invocation/evidence references, outcome, cost reservations and fenced lease; never a raw-audio copy or private chain-of-thought log |
| Completion follow-up | Revisioned item/gap, question and input specification, evidence/benefit references, presentation claim, snooze/dismissal/answer state |
| Operation/change | Idempotent writes, revisions, tombstones and ordered synchronization |

Create one stable capture-item model in the new database, informed by the useful invariants of the existing model, rather than separate knowledge and recommendation databases. Index shelf, supported capability/use-case, location role/scope, source/audience and explicit rating separately from open-ended observations. A broad shelf is not a substitute for these retrieval fields; derived indexes can be rebuilt after taxonomy or model changes without changing the underlying observations. Invalidate affected knowledge projections on item correction/revocation/deletion before new answers use them. Deleting only a retained transcript removes its owner-only source projections and source links, not the independently retained knowledge or its permitted retrieval. If embeddings are made through an external provider, that is a disclosed, independently permitted processing operation; private advice and contacts are not sent to a public search engine. Use Postgres relationships and indexes first; this product does not require a graph database to connect people, experiences and entities. Public identity caching must remain separate from private observations and contacts.

### Local behavior and authentication

Flutter repositories expose effective local state. Use SQLite for structured records, current source text, corrections and an outbox; encrypted or platform-protected app-owned temporary files for pending audio; platform secure storage for credentials. Scope all data and work to a signed-in new-product account. First sign-in requires connectivity; an already authenticated account can capture and browse its own local material offline. A remote authentication failure pauses uploads/AI work and requests sign-in again; it never falls back to a guest owner. Lock local data on sign-out and account switch, stop local account-bound work, and only unlock retained data for the same reauthenticated account. Server processing already authorized is governed by processing permission, not by whether an app window is open or a device signs out. Never upload one account's pending files under another account.

Create a pending-recording record before capture. Finalize/copy into protected temporary ownership and commit the queued record before acknowledging. Filesystem and database writes are not one transaction: reconcile orphaned files, missing files, failed copies and interrupted finalization on startup. Never promise recovery of bytes the OS did not write. Exclude temporary audio from device backups, crash attachments, database blobs and analytics; verify actual native backup rules during the spike.

Own-data browsing and lexical retrieval work offline. Cloud processing and friend retrieval can be unavailable without blocking account-bound text/voice capture. A source transcript is accessible to its owner through detail for inspection, correction or explicit reprocessing; it is not a friend-visible Library item or input to a friend's answer. Owner-only transcript lookup may be added behind an explicit source scope, never merged into the network index. Follow the [Flutter offline-writing guidance](https://docs.flutter.dev/app-architecture/design-patterns/offline-first#writing-data) when distinguishing local acceptance from server acknowledgement.

### Processing permissions and withdrawal

Use these new-product rules rather than legacy opt-out behavior. Record purpose, provider(s), disclosure version, permission generation and the user's choice. These are backend authorization boundaries, not a requirement for five onboarding dialogs: group related disclosed processing choices in simple settings and request optional lookup/discovery at the relevant action. Before first use disclose which processors receive audio or text, why, and their actual retention. Do not silently switch to an undisclosed processor or use product content for training/evaluation. Permission to record, permission to process with a provider and Friends visibility are separate decisions.

| Purpose/control | Permitted work | Effect of turning it off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice transcription | Send the selected audio to the disclosed transcription processor; retain private transcript text | Stop new dispatch, cancel pending transcription and reject late commits; queue unprocessed audio deletion. Preserve transcripts and completed knowledge already saved |
| Knowledge understanding / AI answers | Send required source text or currently authorized item evidence to the disclosed text processor; create knowledge or optional answer synthesis | Stop new understanding/continuation/synthesis and reject late commits. Preserve saved source text, knowledge, corrections and usable local/lexical search |
| Factual completion | Send minimal permitted identity clues to disclosed lookup providers for an existing item | Stop new lookups and reject late results. Preserve saved knowledge; retained provider facts remain only for their permitted lifetime, with no refresh until reauthorized |
| Public discovery | Send a sanitized explicit search to disclosed public providers under its own budget | Stop future discovery and reject late results; existing deliberately kept public leads remain labelled, subject to source retention rights |
| Semantic processing, if enabled | Create/query embeddings of the permitted source scope through the disclosed processor | Stop external embedding work. Keep underlying knowledge; remove remote derived indexes where required by the selected provider/data policy and fall back to lexical search |

Turning processing off is not Delete my memories. Do not cascade permission withdrawal into deletion of completed knowledge or private transcripts. A distinct explicit delete control handles that content; provider-specific derivative expiry cannot erase user meaning or user corrections. Persist saved claims independently of optional indexes, tool caches and provider evidence. Where removal of a provider derivative is required, drop that derivative and its action/source claim without deleting independently retained user material.

After server acknowledgement, withdrawal fences every pending job and result using its permission generation, cancels queued work and prevents further dispatch/commit. Already dispatched requests may be impossible to recall; use provider deletion mechanisms where available and disclose actual retention. Offline, stop new local dispatch immediately and show **Processing change pending** until server acknowledgement; server work may still finish under its last accepted permission. A later opt-in never revives cancelled jobs or reprocesses the old library automatically. A user can explicitly retry retained text under new permission, revision and the same applicable usage limits.

When cloud processing is disabled, existing own-data browsing/search remains available. Remember does not offer a typed fallback. The user can explicitly enable voice processing in settings; recordings must not be dispatched while it is off. Separate underlying transcription and understanding permissions remain useful for cancellation and source recovery, but the ordinary product presents one voice-processing setup choice.

### Audio deletion, retained transcripts and source control

**Retention decision:** retain text transcripts/source text as private user content; do not retain processed audio. Audio deletion follows accepted transcription, not enrichment or successful extraction. A syntactically present but empty/truncated/unusable transcript is not an accepted complete transcription. Retain audio for a bounded transcription retry only while needed; partial transcription can keep unfinished input inside the same window. Once usable text is durably committed, queue deletion of all active app/server audio copies. Extraction failure then retries from text without keeping audio.

| Material | Retention and deletion rule |
| --- | --- |
| Pending audio, not yet acceptably transcribed | Maximum processing eligibility of seven days from capture; earlier Delete or transcription withdrawal triggers deletion. Never extend because a job is stuck |
| Accepted-transcription audio | Queue deletion immediately after durable text commit. Initial server cleanup target: p95 within 15 minutes; any active object remaining after 24 hours is an operational incident. These are targets to verify, not current achievements |
| Local audio | Delete at the next permitted execution after accepted-transcription acknowledgement, explicit deletion, withdrawal or expiry. Retry on startup/foreground. A powered-off or suspended phone cannot promise physical erasure at an exact deadline; expired audio cannot be uploaded or processed when it resumes |
| Retained transcript / typed or shared source text | Owner-only until explicit source/capture/account deletion; preserve through extraction failure or processing opt-out. Store the current revision, not unlimited hidden full-text edit history |
| Knowledge and applied corrections | Retain independently until item/capture/account deletion; neither source deletion nor future-processing withdrawal deletes completed items |
| Tool evidence / provider-derived cache | Retain only as permitted by the actual source; expire independently of user content |
| Backups / object versions | Configure temporary audio outside backups and with object versioning disabled. For text/knowledge backups, choose a maximum 30-day expiry before pilot and verify restore-time tombstone replay prevents deleted data returning to service. Provider copies have separately verified/disclosed limits |

Enforce the audio deadline server-side and locally before dispatch, including clock-skew checks using server timestamps where available. At expiry, stop processing and delete recoverable audio; if no usable transcript exists, show **Not transcribed**. If text was saved but extraction is incomplete, retain it with **Transcript saved — needs processing**; expiration must not discard that text. A transcript is an ASR interpretation, not proof of exact spoken wording. Keep a warning/correction path for transcription uncertainty; there is no audio replay after deletion.

**Source controls:** Transcript is a secondary, owner-only view on capture/item detail, not a required review step. Editing it creates a new current revision and invalidates source-offset links and pending work tied to the prior revision; retain item-level knowledge without presenting stale offsets as current evidence. New extraction is explicit and cannot overwrite manual edits or revive deleted items. Delete transcript removes that source text and source-search projections while keeping completed knowledge; item evidence then says source removed instead of pointing to missing text. Typed/shared source text uses the same owner-only access rules. Do not expose full-capture text, sibling source spans or private referrers through friend evidence links or model context.

**Deletion scopes:** Delete item removes the item, its contacts, indexes, kept references and item-specific source content. Redact confidently isolated source spans and drop superseded copies; if source content cannot be isolated safely in a multi-item transcript, delete that whole transcript after a clear confirmation that other completed items remain. Keep a non-content tombstone so reprocessing cannot resurrect deleted items. Delete capture/thought removes its source text, pending audio and all derived items, with the scope shown before confirmation. Delete account removes all account content and references. Do not offer Undo after irreversible source purge unless the bounded recovery interval and audience behavior were explicitly designed and disclosed; confirmation is sufficient for V1.

Deletion is a durable, idempotent job with retry, object-store confirmation, revision/permission fencing and a sweeper for abandoned uploads, failures and expiry. Server access removal happens at the deletion transaction; physical purge has an initial 24-hour active-store target, escalated if missed, with separately documented backup/provider limits. Source/transcript purge must remove old revisions, projections and queued payloads, not just hide the visible record. Jobs store references rather than copied source text. Restore procedures replay deletion records before serving restored data. Keep minimal receipts without private content.

### Offline visibility and server acknowledgement

Local durability, remote processing and network visibility are distinct states. An offline save can say **Queued on this device**, not **Available to friends**. Send the current desired audience with the capture's initial upload; apply it transactionally when knowledge becomes publishable. A subsequently received capture-level Private choice is applied to every generated item before publication and invalidates already published siblings in the same transaction. Extraction and visibility writes lock/check the current capture revision; stale jobs cannot override a server-acknowledged Private choice.

For an already uploaded capture or item, an offline Private/Delete/revoke operation cannot change what the server currently serves. Queue the change with high synchronization priority and display **Privacy change pending — currently Friends** or **Deletion pending — friends may still see this** as applicable. The existing server audience remains effective until acknowledgement. Hide a locally deleted item immediately if helpful, but retain a visible pending-operation receipt; never claim network removal until server commit. After acknowledgement, new reads/actions must check current access and caches/projections cannot grant permission independently.

Privacy-narrowing writes and tombstones take precedence over stale content updates and background work. Reject stale Friends-widening writes and require deliberate retry against the current revision; a correction, reprocess or merge cannot silently change Private to Friends. New contact/content uploads depend on acknowledgement of a pending privacy restriction so new details are not added to the old audience. Send restrictive operations before dependent outbox writes and handle revision conflicts explicitly. Broadening to Friends, accepting a friendship and deleting an account require an online server acknowledgement before the UI reports success. First-friend acceptance revalidates the reviewed visibility state; if it changed materially, refresh the review.

Friend content requires online validation. Do not persist it as an offline-readable source cache; clear rendered network content when the app loses validation or is backgrounded, revalidate on foreground and before actions, and invalidate active views when revocation is learned. An already displayed screen or external copy cannot be retroactively unseen. Revocation guarantees apply to server-authorized future reads/actions after commit, not instant physical erasure on unreachable devices. Platform background work is best effort; use foreground reconciliation and [Android's persistent-work guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/background-tasks/persistent), without promising exact execution times on suspended devices.

### Initial resource envelope

Initial pilot defaults are configurable hypotheses: at most five minutes and 20 MiB per voice capture, 20,000 Unicode characters per typed/shared-text capture, 50 MiB of locally pending audio per account/device and 15 accepted audio minutes per account/day. Validate MIME/decoded duration as well as declared length. Show a limit before accepting unsupported input; at recording duration/size limits finalize the recorded portion visibly and offer a new capture, never silently truncate. If local storage is full, offer removal of identified pending recordings without deleting saved knowledge; do not add a typing chooser. Account-level request, upload and paid-usage limits are enforced server-side across devices, with separate IP/device abuse limits for authentication and verification.

A normal understanding response targets at most 20 items. For larger useful content, persist explicit partial/overflow state and the complete text source; use bounded segmented continuation (initially at most two additional understanding calls within the same creation budget), or defer the rest visibly for an explicit retry. Completed initial segments do not make the whole capture complete. If coverage remains incomplete, keep the new capture's extracted items owner-only and show why; manual correction or finishing the remaining text can complete it. Do not discard claims to satisfy an output schema.

Before any paid pilot, configure transcription-minute, understanding-token, Ask-request and all-stage account/project money limits from measured provider routes; startup must fail for paid features with missing caps. Completion limits in section 6 remain nested within those overall limits. Values may change only with measured quality, peak-load and cost evidence recorded in the tracker; they are not unlimited promises.

### Synchronization

Use a transactionally ordered per-owner change feed. Serialize sequence allocation for an owner in the same transaction as mutations/change records; a plain global sequence can expose a later commit before an earlier allocated change. Deliver every entry, contact, correction, membership, completion dismissal and deletion mutation. Apply a page and advance its persisted device cursor atomically. Define a tombstone retention window and snapshot rebootstrap for devices behind that window.

Use bounded retries, idempotent operation IDs, expected revisions and durable outbox records. User changes outrank regenerated fields; conflicting user edits need an explicit, narrow resolution. Push/SSE may accelerate visibility but cannot replace replayable synchronization. Visible capture detail uses at most one polling request at a time with backoff and stops on dismissal/backgrounding.

### Completion jobs and contracts

Persist one completion operation transactionally with each extracted item and its first decision, keyed by owner, item revision, pipeline version and consent generation; deduplicate explicit retries by operation ID. Record already-finished or needs-user outcomes immediately and enqueue only executable work or a justified continuation. Extend durable Postgres jobs with fenced leases, bounded concurrency, backoff and terminal recovery for exhausted leases. Checkpoint model decisions and tool observations before advancing. Keep processing state (`pending`, `running`, `done`, `failed`, `deferred`) separate from the run outcome (`actionable`, `useful_as_saved`, `needs_user`, `unresolved`). A run awaiting an answer releases its lease and does not accrue polling/model cost.

Recheck current ownership, source/item/correction revisions, deletion and processing permission before every dispatch and result commit. Withdrawal follows the explicit rules above: stop new work and reject late results, preserving saved knowledge and transcripts. New permission does not reactivate cancelled jobs. A correction invalidates affected work and creates at most one new operation within the remaining budget. Persist attempts/reservations before dispatch; uncertain provider completion after a crash is not a free retry. Use provider idempotency where supported and otherwise count possible duplicate calls against the same allowance.

Extend the versioned capture API contracts with flexible observations, evidence-linked completion results, run summaries, follow-up input specifications and revision-checked answer/snooze/dismiss writes. The internal understanding contract includes per-item initial decisions and an explicit partial/overflow result, with independent knowledge/action validation. Keep internal agent/tool traces out of the mobile API. GETs only read saved state; explicit POST operations initiate completion, claim a follow-up for display, or submit an answer. Reuse asset authorization for short voice answers and item-scoped contact assignment for attached contacts. App restarts and multi-device replies must not lose the original knowledge or overwrite a newer correction. Do not queue paid work for all existing items merely because this feature ships.

### Scaling the processing workload

Run separately bounded transcription, understanding and completion worker pools over durable jobs; scale each pool against queue age, provider quotas and latency measurements. Keep provider calls outside database transactions and release workers while waiting for the user. Enforce per-owner fairness, capture-level fan-out limits and provider request/token concurrency limits. Retry transient failures with backoff and jitter; pause an unhealthy provider to prevent retry storms. Prioritize new captures and explicit user actions over optional deeper enrichment. Preserve immediate local capture and display saved advice when enrichment is delayed.

Reuse confirmed identities and cache public evidence only where the source permits it. Coalesce duplicate eligible lookups, with an identity- and policy-aware cache key; do not key a match on a common name alone or let a cache expose private advice/contacts between owners. A shared public entity can have many independently attributed experiences. Object storage holds temporary audio; jobs carry references and compact checkpoints. Database indexes and the owner change feed serve retrieval/sync without scanning the full library or launching research when it is opened.

Measure database locking in shared budget reservations and owner change allocation under peak load. If a global ledger becomes a bottleneck, use transactionally bounded reservation allocations whose total cannot exceed the ceiling. Postgres in the new backend is the initial design; benchmark realistic recording sizes, item counts, bursts and provider failures before asserting a scale limit or adding infrastructure. Audio expiry/deletion and retention-aware cache cleanup remain required at every scale.

### Where Rust may change performance

Rust is the chosen backend implementation language. Its main potential gains are lower per-process memory and more predictable CPU use under concurrency, particularly when many capture jobs perform local media validation or transformation, transcript segmentation/deduplication, evidence parsing, reranking, embedding or index-preparation work. Bounded Rust worker pools can also make queue admission, cancellation and lease fencing efficient without adding more processes. These are engineering hypotheses, not promised user-visible speedups; add the relevant work only when its slice calls for it.

For the first text item and own-Ask path, Postgres queries and indexes dominate most server work, so changing the HTTP language alone is unlikely to make a large difference. For voice understanding and completion, remote transcription/model/lookup latency, provider quotas and retries dominate. Rust does not improve a model's extraction quality, retrieval relevance, device recording behavior, or the privacy rules. Optimize SQL plans, result limits, batch boundaries and provider calls before adding native search or media infrastructure. Prefer mature Rust crates and isolated adapters for provider integrations; do not hand-roll JWT verification, audio codecs or cryptography for a presumed speed gain.

At each workload gate, measure API and worker p50/p95/p99 latency, throughput, CPU, resident memory, database time/locks, provider wait time, queue age and cost at realistic concurrent owners, item counts and recording sizes. Include cold start and failure/retry load. Compare changes on identical fixtures and hardware, and keep quality, authorization, audio deletion and cost gates fixed. If a hot path is mostly external wait, prefer concurrency control or provider changes to low-level optimization. Do not claim Rust is faster for Rekky until these measurements show where it matters.

### Sharing boundaries

Temporary audio, private retained source text, completed knowledge, visibility and collection membership are separate concepts. After the account disclosure is accepted, completed items default to Friends; raw source text and pending/failed/known-partial extraction do not publish. Collection moves never change visibility. An acknowledged Private/Delete or friendship change changes current access without editing every derived view; queued offline changes have the explicit pending states above. Future curated views cannot widen audience or reveal transcripts.

Apply access checks before retrieval, ranking, counts, generated summaries and previews. Shared-network caches and generated answers must support invalidation after access changes. V1 keeps own material available offline; shared-network content requires online access validation rather than promising offline access and immediate remote revocation simultaneously. Keep only independently authorized retained material. Recipients may still remember or externally copy content already shown; revocation cannot retract that.

  lib/features/library/
  lib/features/ask/

Use immutable state and injected repositories, with one presentation-state approach across features. Default to SDK ViewModels/Listenable primitives for the initial slice; adopt an additional state package only if a concrete requirement warrants it. Use named/deep-linkable routes; `go_router` is the proposed routing package, subject to compatibility verification when pinning dependencies. Avoid a service locator full of global mutable state.

Version a language-neutral API schema for the new backend in `contracts/rekky/` in this repository. Generate Dart transport models/client code where reliable. Existing legacy Zod contracts are references, not the new API contract; their refinements and runtime semantics mean exporting a schema alone is insufficient. ### How we build with coding agents

Build **front end and backend together in complete, reviewable vertical slices**. Do not finish every Flutter screen against mock data before starting the API, and do not build a speculative backend without testing the mobile journey. Front-loaded work is limited to the focused Flutter walkthrough, native feasibility, small shared contracts and early labelled evaluation fixtures; expand the broader end-state walkthrough during live slices. In parallel, establish the isolated backend and a small versioned wire contract. Replace preview data with real state slice by slice; no preview-only screen counts as implemented.

The sequence is: (1) agree on the journey, navigation and contract examples while proving record/finalize/delete on physical phones; (2) ship a real signed-in voice-or-text capture → distilled item → own Ask loop, including temporary-audio deletion and retained-source controls; (3) make one place actionable and one service contact-completable; (4) add reciprocal friends and an authorized friend Ask loop; (5) deepen retrieval, enrichment and polish only after the basic loops work. Design, backend work and native feasibility may run concurrently where their interfaces are settled, but each slice ends with integrated Flutter/API/database tests and a device check. Keep unfinished or paid features behind new-product flags so one incomplete module cannot block saving or leak into the old app.

Use coding agents for **bounded, independently checkable assignments**, not separate long-running “build the entire frontend” and “build the entire backend” streams. An integration owner maintains the current contract, slice acceptance criteria and assembled journey; agents can take non-overlapping work such as native recording lifecycle, a transcription adapter, a database migration, friend-access rules or an Ask evaluation set. Give each assignment exact inputs/outputs, files or module ownership, fixtures, failure cases and tests. Parallel work should use stable contract fixtures or explicit mocks; do not have agents concurrently rewrite the same contract or shared module. Integrate small changes continuously, run both sides' checks, and review the actual app on devices and realistic voice examples before moving to the next slice. ### Evaluation starts in F-00

Build an initial access-controlled set of 60 independently authored capture scenarios before finalizing extraction/index fields: 40 English/Hindi/Hinglish audio scenarios and 20 typed/shared-text scenarios. ### Correctness gates

- No loss of completed knowledge saves in the supported interruption/restart matrix. A queued recording is explicitly pending, has an honest expiry/retry path and is never mislabelled as completed knowledge.
- Accepted-transcription audio is queued for deletion independently of extraction. Failed/expired audio becomes unusable for processing and is purged within the stated server/local-execution rules; orphan cleanup and backup exclusion are verified. Private transcripts persist until explicit deletion, never enter network views, and source/account deletion removes active copies, revisions and projections with verified backup expiry.
- Processing withdrawal preserves completed knowledge, retained transcripts and explicit corrections; it fences new/late paid work without reviving cancelled operations on re-enable. Signed-out capture/Ask/upload is rejected; account switching cannot transfer pending data.
- Original meaning, attribution, negation, uncertainty and important caveats survive compact display and answers. In labelled restaurant, service, product and open-ended notes, each material assertion is retained as an observation or in the faithful account, or extraction is explicitly partial; filler may be discarded. Measure omission rate separately from identity-match precision.
- A saved person or place has one stable primary shelf across reruns, city changes and new use-case facets; search can find a tailor for supported denim repair without duplicating or moving the item. Unsupported broader capabilities are not asserted as experience.
- Venue city, stated service area, past-job location, comparison mention and capture-location hint remain distinct. Strict city requests exclude other cities; an unknown provider service area never becomes confirmed local merely from a nearby past job or the asker's current location. Manual city and All locations remain usable with denied location permission.
- Your Library contains only created or explicitly kept items; the wider network is a separate authorized browse/Ask scope. Keeping preserves source attribution and access requirements, and revocation removes inaccessible content from the Library view and its indexes/previews.
- No wrong-person contact attachment; no positional reassociation of contacts/corrections on rerun.
- After the account disclosure is accepted, completed items default to Friends without a Share step; Private, pending/failed/known-partial work, retained sources, raw audio, capture hints and referrer contacts remain excluded. Confirmed provider routes accompany their Friends item. New friendships expose prior Friends items. Private/Delete/removal revoke future authorized access on server acknowledgement; offline actions show pending state and cannot claim earlier prevention. Test no transcript/sibling-text leak through evidence links, indexes, prompts or kept references. Nothing is published to the open web.
- An account created in the new app begins with no old-product content or connections, including when the same Google/Apple identity is used. New-account search, jobs, caches, invites and contacts cannot resolve old-product identities or rows; the existing app and its data remain unchanged.
- No connection is created from Google/Apple sign-in, a selected phone contact, a matched account or a sent invite alone. Only reciprocal acceptance grants friend access; blocked/undiscoverable users are not exposed through search, counts or invite errors. Contact permission denial never blocks saving or connection by handle/link, and no broad or nonmember contact graph is retained.
- Before an external friends pilot, item/contact/account reports and blocking work from the native UI; a sensitive contact can be suppressed from network actions during review, and a reviewer can correct or remove it without exposing unrelated private content. Review ownership and response targets are documented and staffed; stale Ask/index/cache/kept references do not keep serving restricted material.
- Account deletion is discoverable in-app and via the required web request path; identity recheck and final confirmation precede it, access is revoked at server commit, and bounded deletion of new-product content, retained transcripts/source text, contacts, pending media and derived state completes despite worker failures. Deleted contributions disappear from friends' results and kept references. Disclose backup/provider retention accurately and prove the old product remains untouched.
- Before entering the signed-in product and again at first friend acceptance, explain earlier Friends items and attached provider contacts. An affirmative account action is required; declining does not create a guest library. First-friend Not now leaves own-account use available. Review what friends will see reflects server-current visibility and excludes private sources. Directory/lookup/invite tests cover minimal profiles, verified identifier changes, nonmember disposal and blocked/undiscoverable inference resistance.
- Every generated answer claim links to accessible supporting evidence; unsupported comparisons/consensus are rejected.
- Ask routes exact recall, constrained recommendation, suitability, comparison and broad planning through measured paths; no mandatory model or agent call is used for an exact supported result. Lexical and semantic candidates honor identical access and hard-location constraints before ranking, and a high similarity score cannot make an unproven capability or contradicted caveat true.
- Every found result that clears the evidence and relevance bar is reachable in the current Ask scope, even beyond the initial viewport or ranking window; pagination neither silently truncates nor repeats results. Confirmed-entity grouping retains every distinct attributed experience; same-name people do not merge. Uncertain or unknown-area leads are separated from confident results, and result counts do not leak inaccessible or unexamined items.
- For an Ask-specific held-out set separate from the capture corpus, report recall at the shortlist, precision of the top result, grounded-answer/action correctness, abstention quality, p50/p95 latency and cost by query class and language. Compare lexical-only, hybrid, reranker and Jev variants at equivalent source scope; adopt additions only on measured improvement. Predeclare per-class pilot thresholds rather than masking weak classes with one average.
- Zero unauthorized search results, counts, snippets, embeddings served to the wrong scope or stale answer actions in the adversarial access suite; wrong-city, wrong-person, unsupported-skill and invented-contact cases remain explicit go/no-go failures. Check corrections and revocations against every index/cache and repeat with approximate vector search if introduced.
- Collection moves do not change audience; overrides and tombstones survive synchronization and regeneration.
- No unauthorized result through search, generated summaries, counts, thumbnails or cached previews.
- No public result is presented as a personal or network recommendation; public discovery respects its own opt-in, source, freshness and spend limit.
- No lookup without applicable consent or after exhausted budgets; late results are fenced by revisions and consent generation.
- Completion-agent evaluations cover choosing no tool, public lookup, clarification or stopping on insufficient evidence; private/public/online services with the same broad category must lead to appropriate different actions. Never invent a numeric rating or turn an inferred trait into a user-supplied fact.
- Combined understanding and first decisions must meet the extraction/identity gates against a separated baseline. Record one-call completion rate, bounded overflow/escalation and full cost; additional calls are allowed when measured quality needs them. Malformed optional actions cannot discard valid advice. Report preservation of meaning against original audio as well as transcript coverage; valid JSON and model self-review are not correctness evidence.
- Inject misleading page instructions, irrelevant candidates, city/branch/type contradictions, repeated tool calls, malformed decisions, response-body timeouts, model timeouts, lease expiry and concurrent budget claims. Prove that unsupported actions are rejected, costs remain bounded and one failed optional completion cannot hide saved sibling knowledge; known incomplete capture extraction remains owner-only until coverage is resolved.
- Follow-ups survive logout/restart, deduplicate across devices, respect snooze/dismissal and become obsolete when corrected, resolved or deleted. Login/follow-up selection creates no new paid operation; already authorized pending captures can still finish processing. Answering a contact question never triggers an unrelated lookup or reattaches a referrer as a provider.
- Meet the staged dataset and automatic-identity confidence/coverage requirements above. Record the actual available scenario, audio and independent held-out match counts rather than assuming the planned 300-case corpus exists. Organization, extraction and Ask have separate metrics; identity precision alone proves none of them.

### Performance and costs

Measure sign-in completion, capture tap→record start, Done→local-queue acknowledgement, upload/transcription→durable transcript, audio purge lag, understanding, first useful destination, local retrieval, first validated Ask result, synthesis and actual spend. Keep earlier latency targets as hypotheses: acknowledgement p95 under one second after finalization, extraction p95 under three seconds after transcription and first useful destination p95 under five seconds after extraction on healthy connectivity. Report total user-perceived time, audio byte-hours, retained-text storage and whether deletion/retrieval targets were actually met. Set Ask targets from measured exact/hybrid/broad paths without hiding search time behind animation.

Track successful later retrieval and useful actions, organization corrections, optional-question dismissal, capture abandonment, contact completion and sharing usefulness. For the completion agent, measure unnecessary-tool rate, useful-action coverage, wrong-match rate, follow-up benefit and abandonment, tool/model call counts and actual completion spend per capture/category. Raw save counts and outbound clicks alone do not prove value. Keep raw audio, full transcripts, contact values and private content out of analytics.

For the fresh-start loop, measure time and completion rate from first open to first **usefully processed** save, then whether zero-friend users later recover or act on their own knowledge. Separately measure the path from an optional find/invite action to an accepted first friendship and to a genuinely useful friend-sourced Ask result; invitation count by itself is not success. Track first-connection disclosure comprehension, processing-off/Private/Delete rates shortly after connecting, contact-permission denial with successful handle/link fallback, and reports of unexpected visibility as trust guardrails. Measure returning use over weeks for solo and connected cohorts, with source-labelled useful-action coverage rather than raw network size. Set pilot thresholds after baseline/usability evidence, and collect aggregate events without raw note, query, contact or invite-recipient content.

Track the fraction of captures completing with one understanding call, continuation/escalation rate, p50/p95/p99 queue and stage latency, actual input/output tokens, duplicate lookup avoidance, retry spend and total creation cost per capture and saved item. Set the production average-cost target from the measured recording length, number of subjects and lookup mix; the $0.10 ceiling is not that target. Request higher provider quotas before expected peaks exceed them, and verify graceful delay under load rather than promising a fixed enrichment latency.
